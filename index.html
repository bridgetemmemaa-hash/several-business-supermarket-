A vision for everyday essentials

More than a supermarket.

A neighborhood store can be the starting point for a whole family of businesses—bringing useful services, fresh products, and new opportunities together.

Business ideas to grow into

Build at a pace that works for you. Start with the core store, then explore the opportunities that fit your community.

🛒01

Supermarket & grocery retail

Offer groceries, household goods, personal care, and daily essentials in one convenient place.

📦02

Wholesale & distribution

Buy in larger quantities and supply local shops, food businesses, and community buyers.

🥖03

Bakery & prepared foods

Make fresh bread, pastries, and ready-to-eat meals for shoppers looking for convenience.

🥬04

Fresh produce & local sourcing

Sell fruits and vegetables while building relationships with nearby farmers and producers.

🚚05

Online ordering & delivery

Let customers shop remotely, arrange home delivery, or collect an order from the store.

☕06

Café or juice bar

Create a welcoming spot for a quick drink, snack, or relaxed break during a shopping trip.

🧴07

Personal care & wellness shop

Expand the selection of beauty, hygiene, baby-care, and other everyday wellness products.

💳08

Convenience services

Add useful extras such as mobile top-ups, bill payment, parcel pickup, or a cashpoint, where available.

🏷️09

Private-label products

Develop a house brand for selected items, giving customers another option and building a recognizable identity.

💡

A smart way to begin: learn what local customers need most, check costs and regulations, and grow one idea at a time.

Good business grows from understanding the community it serves.
